\section{Backbone transfer}
\label{app:backbones}

Tables~\ref{tab:headline-clipl} and~\ref{tab:headline-clipl-a005}
repeat the comparison of Table~\ref{tab:headline-main} on CLIP
ViT-L~\citep{radford2021clip} embeddings of dimension $d = 1024$, at
both miscoverage levels and on the budget grid $s \in \{2, 4, 8\}$ run
for this encoder. The pipeline and per-dataset configuration of
Appendix~\ref{app:config} carry over unchanged. Only the pool
statistics are refitted, and the softmax temperature is re-piloted for
the encoder. FRCP is smallest in 7 of 9 cells at $\alpha = 0.1$ and 8
of 9 at $\alpha = 0.05$, and the EuroSAT crossover of the main table
is again the only exception. The advantage also widens with encoder
quality: on the smaller CLIP ViT-B the corresponding counts are 6 of 9
at both levels, and the FRCP sets themselves roughly halve on
CIFAR-100 in the move to ViT-L, 21.1 to 11.7 at two shots.

\section{Refinement and the score distribution}
\label{app:scorehist}

Figure~\ref{fig:score-hist} shows what the frozen refinement does to
the distribution of the deployed prototype-softmax score. Because the
piloted temperatures are sharp, the score saturates near 0 and 1, so
the axis is logit-scaled and both saturated ends are resolved. Before
refinement the false-class mass leans on the calibration quantile from
above and spills across it. After refinement the true-class mass
concentrates well below the quantile and the false-class mass
separates cleanly above it on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100, which is why the
same coverage is bought with fewer false labels in the set. On EuroSAT
the two masses stay entangled and the gain is correspondingly small,
consistent with its position in Table~\ref{tab:headline-main}.

\begin{figure}[h]
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{fig_refine_score_hist_softmax.pdf}
\end{center}
\caption{Refinement and the score distribution. True-class (green) and
false-class (red) mass of the deployed prototype-softmax score
$s = 1 - p_T(c \,|\, z)$ over the test set, before (top) and after
(bottom) the frozen refinement T$\to$W$\to$S, with the calibration
quantile $\hat q_{1-\alpha}$ dashed, at four labels per class and
$\alpha = 0.1$ on a single balanced split (DINOv2 ViT-B, per-panel
pilot temperature annotated). The score axis is logit-scaled to
resolve both saturated ends, and bar heights are per-class probability
mass per bin. Annotations give coverage, mean set size, and leak, the
mean number of false labels inside the set.}
\label{fig:score-hist}
\end{figure}
