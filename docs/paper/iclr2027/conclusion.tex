\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduce FRCP, which gives each of its three inputs one job. A
frozen self-supervised backbone supplies the representation, so nothing
is trained on the task. The unlabeled pool refines that representation
through truncation, whitening, and smoothing. The labeled sample is
reserved entirely for conformal inference, which full conformal
prediction allows by scoring candidate labels against class prototypes
rather than a trained head, at closed-form cost. Across the benchmarks
and label budgets we study, FRCP returns the smallest prediction sets in
the regime it targets, with the largest gains at the lowest budgets and
diminishing gains as labels accumulate.
